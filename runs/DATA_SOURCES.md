# Public NNUE training data survey (research agent, 2026-09-16)

## Public NNUE training data survey (verified 2026-09-16)

Everything below was checked against the live HuggingFace tree API, the lczero storage index, Kaggle's public API, or the raw README/source, today. Sizes are bytes reported by those APIs, shown in GB (10^9). "Could not verify" is stated wherever I could not confirm a claim from a primary source.

### How to read position estimates

Binpack density depends on encoding, not content. Measured on the same T91 data by adamtwiss ([README](https://huggingface.co/datasets/adamtwiss/t91-binpacks-filtered)): full-position form 18.2–26.2 bytes/pos; delta-encoded (`minimize_binpack`) form 2.3–2.8 bytes/pos, with no positions lost. `.zst` shrinks full-form binpacks ~1.9x (xushawn: 59.44 GB → 31.18 GB) but minimized binpacks only ~1.2x (linrock 2024-01 min-v2.v6 .zst 7.69 GB vs the same file uncompressed at 9.30 GB in xushawn/test80-bt4-relabel). So the "~20-25 bytes/pos, 1.15x" rule of thumb is only right for full-form files. Estimates below use 22 B/pos (full form), 3-5 B/pos (minimized/filtered; the T91 measurement gives 2.84 but older linrock filters may have physically removed positions, which breaks chains) and are marked "est.". Stockfish self-play (gensfen) binpacks are game-chained and therefore also dense; for the four you already hold, `trainer/target/release/inspect stats` gives the exact count and calibrates the rest.

### Suffix decoder (from linrock's tools and file names)

- `16tb7p` / `12tb7p` / `2tb7p`: size of the 7-piece Syzygy set used when rescoring Leela data with the lc0 rescorer (Tilps `rescore_tb`); `69gb6p` (xushawn) = full 3/4/5-piece plus 6-piece WDL-only TBs ([README](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xushawn/t80-binpacks-raw)). The rescorer corrects Q/D with tablebases; the binpack score field is derived from Leela's Q, not from a Stockfish search. The exact Q-to-cp formula could not be verified at the pinned commit (the file `src/trainingdata/rescorer.cc` is not present in xu-shawn/lc0's tree at 6ca985b).
- `v6` / `v6-dd`: linrock's `csv_filter_v6_dd.py` (nnue-data, `master`): early-ply skip 28, drops "one good move" / "only one move" positions, de-duplicates ("dd") on piece orientation. `sk16`/`sk20`: early-ply-skip variants (inferred from name; could not verify). `min` / `min-v2`: Stockfish `transform minimize_binpack` re-chaining (no positions removed); `-v2` difference could not be verified. `no-db`, `3072`, `pdist`, `.q.binpack`: could not verify meaning.
- `relabel-BT4-tf13tune`: score field replaced by the Q of the Leela BT4 transformer net (xushawn README: "Data relabled with BT4 net evaluation Q-value", net link + lc0 commit given). This is what Stockfish's current threat-input nets train on ([vondele/nettest threats.yaml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vondele/nettest/main/threats.yaml)).
- `high-simple-eval-1k`: |simple eval| > 1000 subsets built for the dual-NNUE small net (linrock/dual-nnue, master-smallnet-binpacks). Not useful for a main net.

## Recommendation for a 600 GB box at ~60 MB/s

60 MB/s is 216 GB/hour, so the link is not the constraint; disk and decompression scratch are. Keep the budget for uncompressed working files plus ~100 GB scratch (bullet's sfbinpack loader can take several files, so avoid building one giant interleaved copy; verify the loader's multi-file behaviour locally first).

Suggested additions, in order (all SF binpack, no conversion):

1. **vondele/linrock_relabel_2**, plain (non-`.q`) files only — 141.7 GB, ~40 min. This plus your existing files is the core of the current Stockfish threat-net recipe. (If you would rather keep search labels, take linrock/test80-2023's 12 threats.yaml files instead: 121.5 GB .zst, ~146 GB decompressed — not both.)
2. **adamtwiss/t91-binpacks-filtered** — 12.6 GB, 4 min. Confirm your loader skips score 32002.
3. **xushawn/test80-bt4-relabel** Jan+Feb 2024 plain `.binpack` — 17.7 GB, 5 min; drop your own 2024-01/02 files.
4. **vondele/linrock_relabel_1** test80 2022 Jun-Sep + Nov plain (62.8 GB) — or the linrock .zst equivalents (55 GB .zst). ~20 min.
5. **T60T70wIsRightFarseer.binpack** — 33 GB, 10 min.
6. Optional filler if space remains: **multinet + wrongIsRight + fishpack32** (40.5 GB), **from_kaggle_1** split_0-1 (38.7 GB).

Tally: existing ~217 GB (156.3 master-binpacks + ~61 test80-2024) + 141.7 + 12.6 + 17.7 + 62.8 + 33.0 = **~485 GB**, ~2.3 h to download everything from scratch, leaving ~115 GB scratch. Skip xushawn's raw T80 2024-25 (206 GB .zst, 394 GB raw, unfiltered) unless you drop the 2022 set; it is the largest pool of *new* positions but would need the SF `minimize_binpack`/filter pass on the box to fit.

Caveats worth writing into the run log: (a) T80 2023 exists in four places (linrock, vondele relabel, dual-nnue hse subsets, Kaggle) — never stack them; T60T70wIsRightFarseer overlaps from_kaggle_2 and farseerT74/75/76 by name; (b) BT4-relabelled files carry net-eval labels, not search labels — SF found them better, but that is unverified for a much weaker engine; (c) dfrc_n5000 and PlentyChess data contain Chess960 castling; the linrock Leela conversions had non-standard castling positions removed (`filter_plain.py`); (d) HF anonymous downloads can be throttled — use `hf_transfer` or `huggingface-cli` with a token; (e) the three `.tar.zst` in linrock/test80-2024 for Jul-Sep 2024 are raw Leela chunks and are useless without the rescorer.
